# Steady Days — what's next

## Ideas to build
- **Your tree, quietly drawn** — a small tree in the forest header that grows with you: leaves for days you showed up, roots that thicken with steady habits, a blossom when a vision tile is done. Follows the real seasons. Never wilts or punishes missed days.
- **Rings as the yearly story** — each finished year becomes a growth ring you can tap to open that year's recap (days shown up, moods through the seasons, books/manga/films finished, trips, vision and bucket-list items lived, Japanese progress, a few of your own reflections). Ties into the existing year-end transition.
- **N4 Japanese** — grammar library, Go deeper lessons and readings for the next level.
